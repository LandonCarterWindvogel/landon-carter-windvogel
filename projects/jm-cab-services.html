Work/JM Cab Services

JM Cab Services

A focused transport website that moves a visitor from local search to a booking conversation with as little friction as possible.

Role
Design & development
Stack
HTML, CSS, JavaScript
Focus
Conversion, mobile UX, local search
Status
Live

The challenge

A transport customer usually wants one thing quickly: to know who is available, what the service covers and how to book. The site has to remove friction rather than add more information for the sake of it.

The direction

Compact and action-oriented. Content hierarchy, mobile layout and direct booking pathways are treated as conversion tools, not decoration.

The build

  • A lightweight static frontend with a mobile-first structure.
  • Direct contact and booking pathways kept within thumb reach.
  • No application stack to maintain, so the site stays quick and cheap to run.
