BL&N Electrical & Plumbing

A service-business website built around trust, clarity and helping the right local customer understand what the business can do.

Role
Design & development
Stack
HTML · CSS · JavaScript
Focus
Services · trust · local SEO
Status
Live

The challenge

Communicate electrical, plumbing and property-maintenance services quickly without making the customer dig through a generic brochure-style site.

The direction

The experience puts service clarity, local relevance and strong calls to action at the centre. The visual system is deliberately practical while still giving the business a professional presence that feels more established than a basic template.

The build

The site uses a lightweight frontend with semantic markup, responsive layout primitives, structured content and deployment through Netlify. Local-search foundations and accessibility are considered alongside the visual design.

The result

The finished build is designed to stay fast, readable and maintainable while making the client's most important action obvious. The implementation keeps the technology deliberately lightweight so the interface can evolve without unnecessary application complexity.
