BL&N Electrical & Plumbing

A trades website built around trust, clarity and getting the right local customer to pick up the phone.

Role
Design & development
Stack
HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Netlify
Focus
Lead generation, local SEO
Status
Live

The challenge

Electrical, plumbing and holiday-home maintenance in one business, across several towns. Customers often arrive stressed and in a hurry, so the site has to answer "can you help, where, and how do I reach you" in seconds.

The direction

A "trade docket" concept: practical, structured and unmistakably a working business. The colours are sampled from the real logo, cobalt and amber, so the site and the business feel like one brand.

The build

  • Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No Tailwind, no build step.
  • A tabbed services interface and a filterable gallery.
  • An emergency call-out banner and a dedicated section for holiday-home and Airbnb maintenance.
  • Local SEO foundations: structured data, FAQ schema and copy covering Plettenberg Bay, Knysna, Bitou, Keurboomstrand, Natures Valley and The Crags.
  • Deployed on Netlify with security headers.
