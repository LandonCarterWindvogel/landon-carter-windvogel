Work/Paivepo Art & Decor

Paivepo Art & Decor

A luxury, image-led storefront for African art, decor and handcrafted work, built to let the pieces lead.

Role
Design & development
Stack
Vanilla JavaScript SPA, Netlify
Focus
Ecommerce, art direction
Status
Pre-launch

The challenge

Paivepo sells beaded sculptures, oil paintings, acrylics and barrel furniture from several artists. The site had to feel like a gallery rather than a shop, while still giving each artist and product type a clear place to live.

The direction

The redesign repositions the brand from a single-artist shop toward a luxury African design house. A warm ivory, charcoal and soft-gold palette, Cormorant Garamond paired with Inter, and an eight-section homepage keep the artwork large and the interface quiet.

The build

  • Vanilla JavaScript single-page app with hash-based routing. No framework.
  • Netlify Forms for enquiries, with visible error handling instead of silently swallowed failures.
  • A WebP image pipeline for the artwork.
  • Security headers in netlify.toml and correctly formatted international WhatsApp links.
  • Cart and wishlist kept in the browser, no account needed.

Where it stands

The site is waiting on final content from the client, so it is shown as pre-launch. A working preview is available.
