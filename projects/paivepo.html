Paivepo Art & Decor

A premium, image-led digital experience for African art, decor and handcrafted work — designed to give the artwork room to lead.

Role
Design & development
Stack
HTML · CSS · JavaScript
Focus
Art direction · storytelling · ecommerce
Status
Pre-launch
Project photography and final interface captures will be added as the client-approved content set is finalised.

The challenge

Paivepo needed a digital presence that could carry the atmosphere of the physical art and decor experience while giving multiple artists and product types a coherent place online.

The direction

The visual direction prioritises large imagery, restrained typography and editorial spacing. The interface supports the artwork instead of competing with it, with a clear route from discovery to product information and enquiry.

The build

The implementation is deliberately lightweight, using semantic HTML, modern CSS and vanilla JavaScript. Performance, responsive image handling, accessibility and maintainable structure are part of the foundation rather than post-launch fixes.

What I am documenting

The full case study will be expanded with the final design system, responsive decisions, content architecture, screenshots and the reasoning behind the finished experience once the project reaches launch.
