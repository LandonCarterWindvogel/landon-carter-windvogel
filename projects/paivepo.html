Paivepo Art & Decor

A luxury-oriented web presence for African art, decor and handcrafted work.

Role
Design & development
Stack
HTML · CSS · JavaScript
Focus
Art direction · storytelling
Status
Featured project
Project imagery will be added here.

The challenge

Present a handcrafted art business with enough atmosphere and confidence to feel premium without letting the interface overpower the work itself.

The direction

The case study will document the visual system, content hierarchy, responsive decisions and conversion paths used to turn the catalogue into a more intentional digital experience.

The build

The production implementation stays deliberately lightweight so imagery, typography and content carry the experience rather than a heavy application framework.
