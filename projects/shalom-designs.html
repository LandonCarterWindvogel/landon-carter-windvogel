Shalom Designs

An active ecommerce rebuild focused on catalogue accuracy, clearer product discovery and a more distinctive brand-led shopping experience.

Role
Design & development
Stack
HTML · CSS · JavaScript
Focus
Ecommerce · UX · content architecture
Status
In development

The challenge

Rebuild the experience without losing the depth of the existing catalogue. School uniforms, accessories and additional product categories need to remain discoverable while the overall interface becomes cleaner and more premium.

The direction

The rebuild is being treated as an information-architecture problem as much as a visual redesign. Product categories, school pages, size information and the chatbot experience all need to work together instead of competing for attention.

The build

The implementation stays lightweight with semantic HTML, modern CSS and vanilla JavaScript. Accessibility, performance, SEO and catalogue integrity are being considered throughout the rebuild rather than added after the visual work is finished.
