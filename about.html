About

I care about how a website feels — and how it works.

A developer-led approach to client websites: clear structure, strong visual direction and code that is understandable months after launch.

How I work

I build primarily with semantic HTML, modern CSS and vanilla JavaScript. That keeps the stack small, makes performance easier to reason about and gives every project a clear foundation.

What matters

Accessibility, responsive behaviour, search visibility, performance and security are not finishing touches. They belong in the structure of the site from the beginning.

What comes next

This portfolio is being built alongside real client work. Each completed project will become a deeper case study rather than a gallery thumbnail with a vague description.
